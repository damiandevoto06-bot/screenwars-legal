Privacy Policy

Last updated August 20, 2026

DLC Digital, LLC ("we," "us," "our") operates the Screen Wars mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using Screen Wars, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. Screen Wars is intended for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and provided us information, contact us at \(AppLinks.supportEmail).

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly • Account information: username, password (stored encrypted/hashed, never in plain text), and optionally a phone number. • Profile information: an optional profile picture you choose to upload. • Communications: if you contact us for support, we collect what you send us. Information collected automatically • Screen time / app usage data: Screen Wars uses Apple's Screen Time and Family Controls frameworks, with your explicit permission granted through Apple's own system prompts, to estimate how much time you spend on apps and app categories you choose to track. This data is: – Estimated, not exact (Apple's frameworks provide threshold-based estimates, not precise-to-the-second tracking) – Only for apps/categories you explicitly select via Apple's picker – Synced to our servers only for the purpose of powering the App's social and competitive features (see Section 2) • Device and usage information: general technical information such as device type and app version, for debugging and reliability purposes. Information we do NOT collect • We do not read, store, or transmit the names of specific individual apps you use, or content within those apps. Apple's frameworks do not expose this information to us even if we wanted it. • We do not access your contacts list directly. If you use the "find friends from contacts" feature, your contacts' phone numbers are converted to a one-way cryptographic hash on your device before anything is sent to our servers — we never see or store raw phone numbers from your contacts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to: • Create and maintain your account • Power the App's core features: screen-time tracking display, blocking/limits, friend connections, and competitive features (battles, leaderboards) that compare your synced screen-time data against friends who have also chosen to connect with you • Send push notifications related to app activity (e.g., battle results, friend requests) if you enable notifications • Process subscription payments (see Section 5) • Maintain and improve the App, including debugging and fixing errors • Communicate with you about your account or support requests We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share information only in these circumstances: • With other users, as intended by the App's features: if you connect with a friend, that friend can see information you've agreed to share through the App's features (e.g., screen time in a resolved battle, achievements). You control who you connect with. • Service providers: we use third-party services to operate the App, including: – Supabase (database and backend infrastructure) – Apple (App Store, Family Controls/Screen Time frameworks, push notifications, in-app purchases) – RevenueCat (subscription management) – Email provider (transactional emails, e.g., password resets) Each of these providers processes data only as necessary to provide their service to us, under their own privacy and security obligations. • Legal requirements: if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of DLC Digital, LLC, our users, or the public. • Business transfers: if DLC Digital, LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Your Choices and Rights

• Screen Time access: you can revoke Screen Wars's access to Screen Time data at any time via iOS Settings. Doing so will stop tracking but may affect features that depend on it (blocking, battles, etc.). • Account deletion: you can delete your account at any time from within the App (Profile → Delete Account). This permanently removes your profile and associated data from our systems, subject to any data we are required to retain for legal or accounting purposes. • Notifications: you can disable push notifications at any time via iOS Settings. • Phone number: providing a phone number is optional and only used to help friends find you; you can remove it at any time. If you are a resident of California, the EU/UK, or another jurisdiction with specific data rights (such as access, correction, deletion, or portability), contact us at \(AppLinks.supportEmail) and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

5. Payments and Subscriptions

Screen Wars offers optional paid subscriptions processed entirely through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information — Apple and our subscription management provider (RevenueCat) handle this. We receive limited transaction information (such as subscription status and purchase history) necessary to grant you access to paid features.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our database (Row Level Security). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Screen Wars is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. See the notice at the top of this policy for how to contact us if you believe this has occurred.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through the App. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
