Blocking isn't working

Screen Wars blocks apps through Apple's Screen Time system, which needs your permission to work at all. Open iOS Settings → Screen Time and check that Screen Wars still has access. If permission was denied or later revoked, blocking stops silently.

Blocked apps start each day unblocked and shield themselves once you have used up that app's daily limit — so an app you have not opened yet today will open normally. That is expected.

I can't unblock an app

That is deliberate. Screen Wars has no self-serve unlock: once an app is blocked, only a code from a friend can open it again. Ask a friend from the Blocks tab, and they decide whether to send one.

I'm not getting notifications

Unlock codes arrive as push notifications. If nothing is coming through, open iOS Settings → Notifications → Screen Wars and make sure they are allowed. Codes also appear in your chat with that friend inside the app, so you are never locked out by a notification problem alone.

Password reset emails

Reset emails contain a six-digit code to type into the app, not a link. If one has not arrived within a few minutes, check your spam folder — and make sure you are using the email address your account was created with.

Managing your subscription

Screen Wars Pro is billed through your Apple Account, so cancellation and billing are handled by Apple rather than by us. Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Screen Wars. You can also reach it from Profile → Manage Subscription inside the app.

If you have paid but the app still shows the paywall, use Restore Purchase on the upgrade screen. If that does not resolve it, email us with the Apple Account you purchased on and we will sort it out.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete Account removes your account and its data. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that separately in your Apple Account settings.
